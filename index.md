## CURE Description

A course based research experience is a means of teaching research methods through a combination of lecture, workshop, and hands on experience.  

## Theme & Method

In this course, we will use model-based simulations to evaluate how biases propagate in multi-algorithmic systems. Each student will develop a hypothesis about how multi-algorithmic systems interact with social biases, design an experiment to test their hypothesis, and analyze final results. All students will work on related questions and apply similar methodologies and workshops will allow students to get the benefit of learning together while also leading their own investigation. 

Possible motivating questions that can be refined into more specific hypotheses may include:
- how do different types of bias interact when the output of an algorithmic decision is used in another system? 
- how do different fairness interventions work in multi-step context where future versions are trained using the previous version's decisions
- how well can audits detect biases in multi-algorithmic systems


## Learning outcomes

Upon successful completion of the course students will be able to:
1. Formulate research questions with testable hypothesis
1. Conduct literature reviews
1. Design and conduct empirical Machine Learning research related to fairness
1. Write abstracts and research papers
1. Review research and provide constructive feedback

## Schedule

Each week there will be 2 hours of meeting time. This time will be split into 1 hour lecture and a 1 hour co-working session on most weeks.  Some weeks will be completely workshop. 

This course counts for 2 credits of practicum time as well, so there is more significant work outside of class time than a typical lecture-based course. 

In total, students should expect to spend 10 (2*2+2*3) to 14 (2*3+2*4) per week  on this course on average, though this may vary somewhat week to week.

## Grading

Grade will be S/U scale for completion of intermedite milestones and final project.

To earn a grade of S students must: 
- actively participate in or make up all workshops
- bring required materials to all workshop sessions or complete makeup activity
- complete required revisions for hypotheses and design until accepted by instructor
- execute the experiment as designed
- analyze results or reasons the experiment failed
- complete a final paper adhering the style for venues in ML
- maintain reflection journal
